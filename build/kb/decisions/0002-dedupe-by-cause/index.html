0002 · Duplicate reports share a demonstrated cause

owner mstelm with Pi accepted reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

Replaces: 0001 · Duplicates share a subject line. The accepted historical record is preserved, not rewritten.

Context

The review of #4471 shows a parser problem and a misleading form hint merged into one ticket. Subject lines and browser-based intake tags do not identify the failing path. The running explorer already distinguishes causes by tenant locale, form version and channel, contradicting the old subject-only rule.

Decision

Merge only reports with the same demonstrated cause. Look up the tenant, verify its locale and booking form, and identify the report channel. Use causeOf and sameCause from src/lib/kb/causes.ts for the supported date paths; compare the report body and reproduction with that result. Do not use subject equality or intake tags as proof.

A day-first tenant’s booking-v3 form report belongs to the hint issue; its app/email report belongs to parser. Other supported day-first paths belong to parser. A missing tenant or unresolved cause stays separate pending investigation, even when subjects match. One-day descriptions must remain visible for reproduction rather than acquire an assumed cause.

Consequences

The desk keeps one issue per demonstrated problem and counts unique report IDs, not export rows. It routes new reports to the corresponding parser or hint issue while this review proceeds. Different subjects can merge when their causes agree. Matching subjects stay separate when their causes differ. New or uncertain paths require investigation and may justify a new ticket. Rebuild the views for each new export; do not treat a static count as a live queue.
